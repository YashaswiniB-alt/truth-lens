## 🎯 Problem Statement

With the increasing use of digital communication, users frequently receive unknown links, messages, images, videos, and audio content.

Some of this content may be misleading, spam, fraudulent, or potentially harmful. Users may not always be able to identify suspicious content before interacting with it.

## 💡 Our Solution

**Truth Lens** is a software-based platform designed to help users assess digital content before interacting with it.

The platform allows users to submit different types of content for analysis and provides an indication of whether the content may be suspicious or trustworthy.

## ✨ Key Features

Truth Lens supports multiple types of inputs:

- 🔗 **Links**
- 📝 **Text**
- 🖼️ **Images**
- 🎥 **Video**
- 🎵 **Audio**

The goal is to provide users with a simple way to identify potentially suspicious content and make safer decisions online.
